# DynIs 2.1 (paused, not released)

Release notes drafted for the 2.1 Mac/Spotify work. Not part of 2.0.1; moved out of CHANGELOG.md so Settings → About doesn't list unreleased features.

## 2.1.0 (draft)
- DynIs for Mac (macOS 12 or later, Apple Silicon and Intel). The island sits at the top of the screen on every Space and over full-screen apps, and wraps the notch on MacBooks that have one. Now Playing for Apple Music and Spotify, battery, AirPods and headphones battery, browser downloads, Google Calendar, Keep Awake, Snap, stats, volume and clipboard. Features macOS doesn't allow are hidden there.
- Spotify: connect it in setup or Settings to get Up Next (click a song to play it), like and unlike, shuffle and repeat, seeking, choosing the device that plays, and adding songs to the queue from search. Controlling playback needs Spotify Premium.
- Click or use the arrow keys on the progress bar to seek.
